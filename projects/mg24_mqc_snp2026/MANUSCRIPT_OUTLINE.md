# SNP2026 manuscript outline

The main manuscript is not rewritten until the numerical study is complete.
This outline fixes the intended story and page discipline.

## Physics chain

Recent experimental 24Mg monopole response -> strongly prolate SLy5 and SkM*
HF references -> real-time E0 and E2(K=0) evolution -> reciprocal signed
off-diagonal response -> robustness of deformation-induced mixing across the
two specified EDFs.

## Figures

1. Experimental monopole response with SLy5 and SkM* TDHF on a common energy
   axis and identical theoretical smoothing. No fitted energy shift or scale.
2. SLy5 diagonal E0, diagonal E2(K=0), and signed cross response, using aligned
   axes and minimal annotation.
3. Direct SLy5/SkM* signed cross-response comparison. If the two-page limit is
   tight, combine this with Figure 2 as compact aligned panels rather than
   reducing legibility.

Every figure must fit the manuscript column/page width, use short legends, and
avoid prose inside the axes. The understood digitization-method phrase is not
placed in a figure.

## Result language

Separate EDF-dependent peak/centroid positions, fragmentation, deformation,
and strength from the structural question of whether both EDFs exhibit the
same mixed-mode signature. Claim robustness only across SLy5 and SkM*, and
only if the forward/reverse responses and numerical checks support it.

## Citation budget

Retain at most five central references if needed for the page limit:

1. the user's 2024 TDHF/Sky3D response paper (mandatory);
2. the recent experimental 24Mg monopole paper;
3. the experimental fine-structure/wavelet paper;
4. Chabanat et al. for SLy5;
5. Bartel et al. for SkM*.

The prose should follow the direct, physics-led writing style established in
the QDMin paper: state the observable, the calculation, the numerical evidence,
and the bounded conclusion without generic scene-setting or inflated claims.
